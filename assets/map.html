Forecast record ›

Measured flows and generation are Energinet's real-time data, five-minute readings averaged to hours. They cover both price areas and every cable.

Auction flows are what the day-ahead auction scheduled on DK1's cables (ENTSO-E). That is what the forecast tries to hit; the measured flow drifts from it during the day.

Forecast: one lasso regression per DK1 cable, saved before noon the day before and never changed. Days marked backfilled were made afterwards, with only what was published before each auction. DK2's cables are not forecast.

Cable routes are schematic. Prices are day-ahead, €/MWh.
